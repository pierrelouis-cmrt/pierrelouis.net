Me, Myself, and I

I'm Pierre-Louis, an engineering student, design nerd, and tech enthusiast. I'm always stuck between tech and modern art, between French and English, and I like it. I'm exploring ideas, building solutions, crafting experiences.

By reading the content of this page, you'll learn more about me.

You can also visit my Now Page to know what I'm doing at the moment.
And if you're curious about what I want to become, see my Someday page.

Values ↓

  • Curiosity

I'm 20 and currently studying engineering at Centrale Lyon in Lyon, France.
I'll try my best here to describe myself in an honest and transparent way.

I'm extroverted one day, introverted the other. I really love meeting new people, but I also need my alone time to recharge. I guess this comes from being an only child, and it's now a core part of who I am.

I move between French and English a lot. I pick the one that carries the feeling more honestly. Sometimes the emotion arrives first and language follows, and you can't do anything about it. By the way, I love writing stuff, but most of the time I never get around to it and keep interesting thoughts for myself, which is quite a shame.

I've always been into computers: hardware, software, gaming, the whole thing (literally the definition of a nerd). That curiosity turned into a real love for design, especially web design and tech in general of course. I learned by rebuilding sites I liked and tinkering until things looked right. Back in high school I spent ages on Discord helping random people fix their computer. I probably debugged more for strangers than for my own projects, but that sense of community kept me going (that's passion right here, there's simply no other way to put it).

Lately I've been deep into AI, from language models to image generation. And even a bit of robotics. A big goal of mine is a double degree between Centrale and a university in the UK or the US, then becoming an AI researcher. The ethics and safety side interests me as much as the tech itself. I'm still young, I grow and evolve fast; so seeing the tech around me evolve even faster is really motivating!

Somewhere along the way I got into modern art and architecture. Maybe it's the clean lines. Maybe I'm just trying to find beauty wherever I can. Photography followed as the next logical step. I started taking picture of absolutely every little things I liked.

On a personal note, I'm not perfect. And I guess nobody is. We've all got quirks. I'm trying to learn, experiment, and be a decent person while I figure things out. In a time when AI is almost always better than us in every way, embracing our imperfections is more important than ever. This makes us human.
