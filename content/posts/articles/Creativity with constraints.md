Quick writing whose goal is to add my point of view to [this article](https://stephango.com/style) by Kepano. I would advise anyone reading this to take a look at his post before continuing here.

You can only have a distinctive style only by having constraints that come from them and are durable enough. People tend to follow trends, it's easy and feels quickly rewarding, but the constraints you set for yourself should never come from the outside. You should not borrow them from others: tends evolve too quickly and those constraints easily become an aesthetic, which creates homogeneity, not a personal style. They are also unoriginal, and sometimes too broad to even give your own style a direction.

One more thing I would like to add is that originality doesn't come from a single constraint but from the combination of multiple ones. Your few constraints can be as simple as possible (by simple I mean trivial,  not broad or loose): as long as they come from you, they will give you a style nobody else has. As Kepano said, you can let your constraints change over time, but I think you should still see them as long-term choices, so they have the opportunity to give your style some momentum.
